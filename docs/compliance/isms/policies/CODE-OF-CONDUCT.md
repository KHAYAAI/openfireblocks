# Code of conduct

ISO/IEC 27001:2022 A.5.4, A.6.2. SOC 2 CC1.1.
Version 0.1, draft, 11 October 2026. Owner: CEO. **Approver: CEO. Not yet approved.** Review: yearly.

FORGE builds software that people trust with money. Everyone who works with FORGE is expected to:

1. **Be honest.** Do not overstate what the product does or has been tested to do, to
   customers, investors, auditors or each other. If a claim is not backed by evidence, do not
   make it. If you find you made one, correct it.
2. **Protect what we are trusted with.** Follow the security policies. Keep customers' and
   colleagues' information confidential.
3. **Speak up.** Report security problems, mistakes and concerns, including about the conduct of
   colleagues or managers, to the CEO or, if the concern is about the CEO, to [to complete:
   a board member or adviser]. No one is penalised for raising a concern in good faith.
4. **Avoid conflicts of interest.** Declare any interest that could influence your decisions
   (for example in a supplier, a customer or a competitor). Do not trade on information about
   customers or their transactions.
5. **Refuse bribes and improper gifts.** Do not offer or accept anything that could be seen as
   buying a decision. Gifts above R1,000 are declared to the CEO.
6. **Obey the law.** Including anti-money-laundering and sanctions rules: never help anyone avoid
   the Travel Rule, sanctions screening or the product's controls.
7. **Treat people with respect.** No harassment or discrimination.

Breaches are handled under the disciplinary process in the HR security policy. Everyone confirms
on joining and yearly that they have read and will follow this code.
