# Acceptable use policy

ISO/IEC 27001:2022 A.5.10, A.6.7, A.8.1, A.8.23. SOC 2 CC1.4, CC2.2.
Version 0.1, draft, 11 October 2026. Owner: ISMS owner. **Approver: CEO. Not yet approved.** Review: yearly.

## 1. Scope

This policy covers everyone who works for or with FORGE CUSTODY, and every device, account and
piece of information they use for that work.

## 2. Rules

1. Use company accounts (Google Workspace, GitHub, AWS, Sprinto) for company work only. Do not
   move company information to personal accounts or personal cloud storage.
2. Keep your accounts to yourself. Never share a password, a one-time code, a session or an API
   key, and never use someone else's.
3. Turn on multi-factor authentication on every account that offers it. Use the company
   password manager [to complete: which one].
4. Never put secrets (keys, passwords, tokens, seed phrases, key shares) in code, tickets, chat,
   email, documents, AI assistants or screenshots. If one is exposed, report it at once and
   rotate it (`docs/security/key-rotation.md`).
5. Do not paste *Confidential* or *Restricted* information (see the classification policy) into
   AI tools or online services that are not in the vendor register and approved for that class.
6. Install software only from trusted sources. Keep the operating system and browser updated.
7. Do not try to get around a security control, even to "test" it, outside an agreed test.
8. Report anything that looks wrong: a suspicious email, a lost laptop, an unexpected prompt for
   your code, a mistake you made. Reporting quickly is never punished; hiding it may be.
9. Customers' and prospects' information is used only for the purpose it was given for.
10. Reasonable personal use of a company laptop is allowed if it does not break these rules.

## 3. Monitoring

Company systems log activity for security purposes (for example GitHub audit logs, AWS
CloudTrail, Google Workspace logs, the Sprinto laptop agent). Logs are used to protect the
company and its customers, in line with the privacy policy, not to watch people's work.

## 4. Acknowledgement

Everyone confirms they have read this policy when they join and once a year (Sprinto records it).
