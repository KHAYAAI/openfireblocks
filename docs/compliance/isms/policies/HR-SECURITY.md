# Human resources security policy

ISO/IEC 27001:2022 A.6.1–6.6, A.6.8, A.5.11. SOC 2 CC1.1, CC1.4, CC1.5.
Version 0.1, draft, 11 October 2026. Owner: CEO. **Approver: CEO. Not yet approved.** Review: yearly.

Applies to employees, contractors and anyone else given access to company systems.

## 1. Before joining: screening

People with access to production, release signing, signing-party hosts or Confidential customer
information are screened before access is given, in proportion to the risk and within South
African law (POPIA, Employment Equity Act; with written consent):

- identity verification;
- criminal record check;
- qualification and reference checks for the role;
- credit check only where the role handles funds and the law allows it.

Results are kept as set out in the retention policy. Screening of existing staff with that access
is completed by [to complete: date].

## 2. Terms of employment

Contracts and contractor agreements include: confidentiality during and after the engagement,
the duty to follow the ISMS policies, assignment of intellectual property, the duty to report
security events, and return of assets.

## 3. During employment

- Security awareness training on joining (within 30 days) and yearly, with a record
  (`records/TRAINING-LOG.md`). Engineers also complete secure coding training.
- Policies acknowledged on joining and yearly.
- Managers make sure their people follow the policies.

## 4. Change and termination

The leaver steps in `records/JOINER-MOVER-LEAVER.md` are completed on the last day: accounts
disabled, devices returned or wiped, shared secrets rotated, signing roles reassigned. The
person is reminded in writing that confidentiality continues.

## 5. Disciplinary process

Breaches of policy are handled through the company's disciplinary process, fairly and in
proportion to the breach and its intent. Honest mistakes reported promptly are treated as a
chance to improve controls, not as misconduct.

## 6. Reporting

Everyone reports security events and weaknesses to security@[domain] [to complete] or to the
incident lead directly, as soon as they notice them.
