# Privacy and data protection policy

POPIA (conditions 1–8, s55, s69, s72); GDPR art. 5, 24, 25, 28, 30, 32–35; ISO/IEC 27001:2022 A.5.34. SOC 2 P1–P8, C1.
Version 0.1, draft, 11 October 2026. Owner: Information Officer. **Approver: CEO. Not yet approved.** Review: yearly.

Procedures and notices: `../privacy/`.

## 1. Roles

| Situation | FORGE's role |
|---|---|
| Staff, applicants, prospects, website visitors, customer contacts | **Responsible party** (POPIA) / **controller** (GDPR) |
| Personal information in a FORGE-hosted customer environment (for example Travel Rule data, users of the console) | **Operator** (POPIA s20–21) / **processor** (GDPR art. 28), acting on the customer's documented instructions |
| Customer-operated installations | None: FORGE does not receive the data |

## 2. Principles (POPIA's eight conditions)

1. **Accountability.** The Information Officer makes sure these rules are followed and registered.
2. **Processing limitation.** Collect only what is needed, lawfully, and with a lawful basis
   recorded in the register of processing (`../privacy/ROPA.md`).
3. **Purpose specification.** Collect for a defined purpose; keep only as long as the
   retention policy allows.
4. **Further processing limitation.** No new use that is incompatible with the original purpose.
5. **Information quality.** Keep information accurate; correct it when told.
6. **Openness.** A privacy notice on the website; a PAIA manual; notices at the point of collection.
7. **Security safeguards.** The ISMS. Breaches are handled as in the incident response plan,
   including notice to the Information Regulator (POPIA s22) and, where GDPR applies, to the
   supervisory authority within 72 hours (art. 33).
8. **Data subject participation.** Access, correction, deletion and objection requests are
   handled as in `../privacy/DSR-PROCEDURE.md`.

## 3. Privacy by design

New features that touch personal information state what they collect and why, and keep it to the
minimum. The product already does this: KYC references instead of documents for token holders,
Travel Rule data only above the threshold, row-level isolation per customer, no personal
information in logs beyond identifiers.

A data protection impact assessment is done before high-risk processing (`../privacy/DPIA-TRAVEL-RULE-AND-AGENTS.md`).

## 4. Direct marketing

Electronic direct marketing follows POPIA s69 and ECTA: business contacts only on a legitimate
interest basis, every message identifies FORGE and offers a simple way to decline, and objections
are honoured at once (`../privacy/OUTREACH-OPT-OUT.md`).

## 5. Transfers outside South Africa

Personal information is transferred outside South Africa only under POPIA s72 (adequate law,
binding agreement, consent or contract necessity) and, for EU data, under GDPR chapter V
(`../privacy/CROSS-BORDER-TRANSFERS.md`).

## 6. Operators and processors

Suppliers that process personal information for FORGE sign an operator agreement / DPA. When
FORGE acts as an operator for a customer, it signs the customer's DPA or offers its own
(`../privacy/DPA-TEMPLATE.md`).
