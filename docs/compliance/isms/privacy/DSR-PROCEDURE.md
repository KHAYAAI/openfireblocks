# Data-subject request procedure

POPIA s23 (access), s24 (correction and deletion), s11(3) (objection), s69 (direct marketing);
PAIA (Form 2); GDPR art. 12–22. Owner: Information Officer. Version 0.1, draft. Objective O-10.

## 1. Receiving a request

Requests may arrive by any channel. Anyone at FORGE who receives one forwards it to
privacy@[domain] [to complete] the same day. The Information Officer logs it (section 5).

## 2. Steps

1. **Acknowledge** within 3 working days.
2. **Verify identity** proportionately: a request from the email address we already hold is
   usually enough; otherwise ask for something that matches our records. Never ask for more than
   needed.
3. **Decide whose data it is.** If FORGE is only an operator for a customer (hosted platform),
   forward the request to that customer within 2 working days and tell the requester; help the
   customer answer.
4. **Search** Gmail, Google Drive, Sprinto, Stripe, GitHub (issues and commits), and any CRM for
   the person's information.
5. **Act:**
   - *Access:* provide a copy and the information in POPIA s23 / GDPR art. 15 (purposes,
     recipients, retention, source, rights).
   - *Correction:* correct and confirm.
   - *Deletion:* delete unless a legal duty or a legal claim requires keeping it; say what was
     kept and why.
   - *Objection to direct marketing:* stop at once and add to the opt-out list
     (`OUTREACH-OPT-OUT.md`). No other reason is needed.
   - *Objection on other grounds, restriction, portability (GDPR):* assess and answer.
6. **Answer** within **30 days** (PAIA allows one extension of up to 30 days with notice;
   GDPR allows two further months for complex requests, with notice within the first month).
   No fee for POPIA/GDPR requests; PAIA fees only as prescribed.
7. **Refusals** state the reason and the right to complain to the Information Regulator (or the
   EU/UK authority).

## 3. Product records

Records in the custody platform (transfers, approvals, Travel Rule) are append-only and subject
to the customer's legal retention. A deletion request for them is answered by the customer, who
may refuse on that ground for the retention period.

## 4. Third parties' information

Before disclosing, remove other people's personal information unless they consent or it is
reasonable to disclose (PAIA s63-style protection).
