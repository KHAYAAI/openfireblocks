**FORGE CUSTODY privacy notice**
Last updated: [date]

This notice explains how FORGE CUSTODY [legal name, registration number] ("FORGE", "we") handles
personal information about people who visit our website, who we contact about our services, who
work for our customers and suppliers, and who apply to work with us. We follow the Protection of
Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA) and, where it applies, the EU and UK General Data
Protection Regulation (GDPR).

**Who is responsible.** FORGE is the responsible party (controller). Our Information Officer is
[name], reachable at privacy@[domain] [to complete], [postal address].

**What we collect and why**

| What | Why | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Your name, job title, business contact details and our correspondence, if we contact you about our services or you contact us | To discuss whether our custody software fits your organisation | Our legitimate interest in offering our services to organisations; you can object at any time |
| Contact and contract details of people at our customers and suppliers | To perform our contracts, support you, bill you, and tell you about security matters | Contract; legitimate interest |
| Information you send through our website [and technical data such as IP address: describe analytics or say none] | To answer you and run the website | Legitimate interest; consent for non-essential cookies |
| Applicants' CVs and, for roles with access to systems that protect money, background checks | To recruit, and to protect our customers | Contract steps; legal justification and your consent for criminal record checks |

**Our software.** Our customers usually run our custody software themselves. In that case we do
not receive the information processed in it. If we host the software for a customer, we act as
their operator (processor) under a written agreement, and the customer's own privacy notice
applies to that information.

**Who we share it with.** Service providers who help us operate (email and documents: Google;
billing: Stripe; cloud hosting: Amazon Web Services; compliance tooling: Sprinto) under contracts
that require them to protect it, and authorities where the law requires. We do not sell personal
information.

**Transfers outside South Africa.** Some of these providers store information outside South
Africa. We transfer it only where POPIA section 72 allows (for example, under binding agreements
that give protection substantially similar to POPIA) and, for EU/UK information, with appropriate
safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

**How long we keep it.** Only as long as needed for the purpose or as the law requires. Contact
details of people we approached about our services are deleted when they ask, or two years after
our last contact. Contract records are kept for five years after the contract ends.

**Security.** We protect information with access controls, multi-factor authentication,
encryption in transit, and the security programme we apply to our own product.

**Your rights.** You may ask to see the personal information we hold about you, have it corrected
or deleted, object to our processing it (including for direct marketing, which we will stop at
once), and, under GDPR, ask for it to be restricted or given to you in a portable format. Write
to privacy@[domain]. We will answer within 30 days. Access requests may also be made under our
PAIA manual [link].

**Complaints.** Please tell us first. You may also complain to the Information Regulator
(South Africa), https://inforegulator.org.za, or, if you are in the EU or UK, to your data
protection authority.

**Changes.** We will post changes here and update the date above.
