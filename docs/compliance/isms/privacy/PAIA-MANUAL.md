**Planned transborder flows:** some operators store information outside South Africa (for
example in the United States or the European Union), under the conditions of POPIA s72.

**Security measures:** a documented information security management system aligned to
ISO/IEC 27001, including access control, multi-factor authentication, encryption in transit,
logging, supplier assessment and incident response.

## 9. How to request access
Complete Form 2 (Request for access to a record of a private body), available from the
Regulator's website, and send it to the Information Officer. Give enough detail to identify the
record and your identity, say how you want access, and, if you are asking on behalf of someone
else, provide proof of that capacity. Fees are prescribed by regulation; the Information Officer
will tell you if a fee is payable. We will decide within 30 days, which may be extended once by
up to 30 days in the circumstances PAIA allows. A refusal will give reasons; access may be refused
on the grounds in Chapter 4 of Part 3 of PAIA (for example the privacy of third parties, commercial
information, or the safety of individuals and property).

## 10. Remedies
If you are not satisfied with a decision, you may complain to the Information Regulator within
180 days of the decision, or apply to court.
