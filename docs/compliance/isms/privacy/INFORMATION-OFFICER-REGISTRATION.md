# Information Officer registration: checklist

POPIA s55–56; PAIA s17 (deputies); the Information Regulator's guidance note on Information
Officers. Owner: CEO. *Steps reflect the Regulator's process as generally described; check the
Regulator's website for the current portal and form before starting.*

## Who

Under POPIA the head of a private body is its Information Officer by default (for a company,
the CEO or equivalent). The head may authorise another person in writing. Deputies are optional
for a small company.

## Steps

1. [ ] Decide who the Information Officer is (and any deputy). If not the CEO, sign a written
       authorisation (delegation).
2. [ ] Gather: company registration number and name, physical and postal address, the
       Information Officer's name, contact number and email, deputy details, and the letter of
       authorisation if delegated.
3. [ ] Register on the Information Regulator's eServices portal (https://inforegulator.org.za)
       under the Information Officer registration option, or by the alternative means the
       Regulator publishes.
4. [ ] Save the confirmation and registration number. Record it in `../09-document-control.md`
       (role register) and in the PAIA manual and privacy notice.
5. [ ] Put the Information Officer's contact details in the privacy notice, the PAIA manual and
       the incident response plan (§11).
6. [ ] Brief the Information Officer on their duties: compliance framework, PIA, PAIA manual,
       internal awareness, handling requests and breaches, working with the Regulator.
7. [ ] Update the registration within the period the Regulator requires whenever the person or
       details change.
