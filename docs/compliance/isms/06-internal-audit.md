# 06 Internal audit programme

ISO/IEC 27001:2022 clause 9.2. SOC 2 CC4.1.
Version 0.1, draft. Owner: ISMS owner. Approver: CEO.

## 1. Programme

- **Frequency:** at least once a year, covering every clause (4 to 10) and every applicable Annex A
  control within a three-year cycle. Higher-risk areas (key management, access, change) are
  audited every year.
- **Independence:** an auditor may not audit their own work. In a small team that means an
  external auditor (Sprinto can arrange one, or an independent consultant). Record the auditor's
  name and independence below.
- **Timing:** the first internal audit must be complete, with findings recorded, **before the ISO
  27001 Stage 2 audit**.
