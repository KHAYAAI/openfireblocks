# 07 Management review

ISO/IEC 27001:2022 clause 9.3. SOC 2 CC1.2, CC2.2, CC4.2.
Version 0.1, draft. Owner: ISMS owner. Chair: CEO.

**Frequency:** at least once a year, and before the ISO Stage 2 audit. In the first year, hold it
quarterly while the ISMS is being set up (a short meeting is enough).
