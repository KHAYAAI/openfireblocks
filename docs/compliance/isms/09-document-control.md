# 09 Document control

ISO/IEC 27001:2022 clause 7.5. SOC 2 CC2.1.
Version 0.1, draft. Owner: ISMS owner.

## Rules

1. ISMS documents live in this repository under `docs/compliance/isms/` (and the security
   documents they reference), or in Sprinto. Git history is the version record; Sprinto
   acceptance or a signed approval table is the approval record.
2. Each document states its version, status (draft or approved), owner, approver and date.
3. A document becomes effective only when approved. Drafts are marked **draft**.
4. **Nothing is marked done, appointed or held unless a record shows it.** Unknown facts are
   written as **[to complete]**. (See CA-001.)
5. Documents are reviewed at least yearly, or on significant change. The review is recorded
   even when nothing changes.
6. Records (filled-in templates, audit reports, minutes, training logs) are kept for at least
   **five years**, or longer where law requires (FIC Act records: five years from the end of the
   business relationship or transaction).
7. Documents classified *Confidential* or above are not committed to a public repository.
   While the repository is public, filled-in records containing personal information (names in
   the training log, background-check results, access-review outputs of company systems) are kept
   in Sprinto or Google Drive, **not** in git. Only the empty templates are kept here.
